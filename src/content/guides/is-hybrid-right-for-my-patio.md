## The Hybrid fits you if

- **Your weather changes by the day.** Mesh for warm, buggy evenings; panels for wind, rain and cooler days.
- **You want the most flexibility.** Each layer covers the full opening on its own, so you never get half a screen.
- **You want the opening clear sometimes.** Fold both layers back to their ends for access.

## A layout that works well

Keep the long front side as polycarbonate panels, and put the Hybrid on the two shorter end openings. You get shelter across the front and adjustable ventilation at the ends. Your design visit decides what works for your patio.

## Choose something else if

- **You only need one or the other.** [Hexfold](/patio-screens/hexfold/) or [Clearfold](/patio-enclosures/clearfold/) costs less.
