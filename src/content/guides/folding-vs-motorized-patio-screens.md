Both kinds of screen disappear when you want the view and close when the mosquitoes come out. The difference is how they move, what they cost and what can go wrong.

## How each one works

**Motorized roll-down screens** wind a fabric or mesh screen onto a roller at the top of the opening. A motor drives the roller, so each opening needs power and usually a remote or wall switch.

**Folding retractable screens** like Hexfold are rigid panels that hang from a top track and fold to the side, like a bi-fold door. On Hexfold the weight rides on the top track, so the panels float and nothing drags on the bottom. You open and close them by hand.

## What it costs on a real patio

Take a common Houston patio: 16 × 14 ft, two open sides, 8 ft tall. That is about 240 sq ft of openings.

- Motorized roll-down screens at $40 to $45 per sq ft: about **$9,600 to $10,800**.
- Hexfold at $28 per sq ft: about **$6,700**, installed by our crew with the warranty included.

## When motorized makes sense

If you want push-button convenience, already have power at every opening, or need to reach a screen that is hard to get to, motorized can be worth the premium.

## When folding makes more sense

If you would rather not service a motor, run wiring or keep track of a remote, a folding screen does the same job with fewer parts. Hexfold also pairs with fixed panels cut to the angle, so a gabled or arched opening can be fully enclosed.
