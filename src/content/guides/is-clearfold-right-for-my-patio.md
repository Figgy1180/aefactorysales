## Clearfold fits you if

- **You want to open the patio up.** Fold the panels away for a party, close them when the wind picks up.
- **You want it to work without power.** Everything opens by hand, even in an outage.
- **You want a door into the yard.** A folding wall doubles as wide access.

## Choose something else if

- **That wall never needs to open.** [Clearfixed](/patio-enclosures/clearfixed/) costs less.
- **You want screens when the panels are open.** The [Hybrid](/patio-enclosures/hybrid/) puts mesh and panels on the same opening.

## What you get

| Feature | Clearfold |
|---|---|
| Panel | Polycarbonate sunshade panel, Smokey Gray or Industrial Blue |
| Opening | Up to 5 ft per pull, 10 ft meeting in the middle |
| Wind | Fold away and secure before storms |
| Hardware | High-end handle; water stop included; low 3/8 in track and ramp included on openings 16.5 ft and wider, an add-on quoted at the visit on narrower ones |
| Price | From $40 per sq ft of opening, installed, before sales tax |

Wind figures come from factory test data and are everyday durability ratings, not a hurricane rating.
