## Clearfixed fits you if

- **You want to use the patio in cooler, wetter weather.** The panels block wind and rain while letting light in.
- **Your roofline isn't square.** Panels can be cut into arches and angles to fill gables.
- **You want a fixed wall at the lowest polycarbonate price.** It starts below our folding systems.
- **You want a tint.** Choose Smokey Gray or Industrial Blue.

## Choose something else if

- **You want that wall to open.** [Clearfold](/patio-enclosures/clearfold/) folds by hand.
- **You want airflow with bugs outside.** Pair fixed panels with [Hexfold](/patio-screens/hexfold/) on another side, or choose the [Hybrid](/patio-enclosures/hybrid/).

## What you get

| Feature | Clearfixed |
|---|---|
| Panel | 4 mm polycarbonate, Smokey Gray or Industrial Blue |
| Panel size | Up to 4 × 11.5 ft each, arches available |
| Visible light | 48% transmission (Smokey Gray panel test) |
| Solar heat gain | 0.45 SHGC (Smokey Gray panel test) |
| Wind | Panel withstood 50 mph in our factory's wind tunnel test |
| Price | From $31 per sq ft of opening, installed, before sales tax |

Panel test values are for the panel, not a full installed-system rating. A 3-season room is shaded and sheltered, not air-conditioned.
