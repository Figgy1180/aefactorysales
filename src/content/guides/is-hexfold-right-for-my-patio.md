## Hexfold fits you if

- **You want the view back when the bugs are gone.** Pull it closed in the evening, fold it away on a nice afternoon.
- **You have a wide opening.** One pull covers up to 10 ft; two pulls meeting in the middle cover up to 20 ft, with no post in between.
- **You don't want a motor.** It opens by hand, so it still works in a power outage and there is no motor, wiring or remote to maintain.
- **You like a clean look.** The weight rides on the top track and the mesh pleats up neatly at the end.

## Choose something else if

- **You never plan to open it.** [Fixed Screen](/patio-screens/fixed-screen/) costs less.
- **You want shelter from wind and rain too.** The [Hybrid](/patio-enclosures/hybrid/) adds a polycarbonate layer on the same opening.

## What you get

| Feature | Hexfold |
|---|---|
| Mesh | Honeycomb aluminum, about 115 holes per sq in |
| Frame | Heat-welded PVC-fiber blend, aluminum core, ceramic coating; stainless steel wheels |
| Opening | Up to 10 × 10 ft per pull, 20 ft wide meeting in the middle |
| Wind | Fold away and secure before storms |
| Price | From $28 per sq ft of opening, installed, before sales tax |

Wind figures come from factory test data and are everyday durability ratings, not a hurricane rating. Accessories and other options are extra and are quoted for your project.
