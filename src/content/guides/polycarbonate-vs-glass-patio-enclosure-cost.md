A glass sunroom is priced like a home addition. A polycarbonate enclosure uses the patio roof you already have and fills in the open sides. That is the main reason the price gap is so wide.

## The same patio, priced three ways

Example patio: 16 × 14 ft, two open sides, 8 ft tall. That is 240 sq ft of openings and 224 sq ft of floor.

| Option | How it is priced | Example patio |
|---|---|---|
| 4-season glass sunroom | $200 to $400 per floor sq ft | $45,000 to $90,000 |
| 3-season sunroom | $80 to $230 per floor sq ft | $18,000 to $52,000 |
| AE Clearfixed to Clearfold | $31 to $40 per sq ft of opening, installed | $7,400 to $9,600 |

Sunroom figures are from the HomeGuide cost guide (Oct 2026), applied to 224 sq ft of floor. AE figures use our lowest installed prices on 240 sq ft of openings.

## What you get for the difference

**Polycarbonate is much lighter than glass** at comparable thickness. That is what makes folding panels like Clearfold practical, and why most existing covered patios can carry it without extra structure.

**It flexes instead of shattering.** Polycarbonate is far more impact resistant than ordinary glass, so a stray ball or a falling branch does not leave broken glass on the patio. This is an everyday durability point, not a hurricane rating.

**Tint you can see through.** Our Midnight Smoke panels tested at 48% visible light transmission and a 0.45 solar heat gain coefficient. They cut glare like sunglasses, and you still see the yard.

**It installs faster.** Every AE panel is CNC-cut to your openings, test-assembled in our Houston factory and installed by our own crew within a month of your order.

## The honest trade-offs

- **Glass resists scratching better.** Clean polycarbonate with a soft cloth and mild soap. No abrasive pads.
- **A 3-season room is not air-conditioned.** It is ventilated through screen doors and doors that open on both sides. On the hottest August afternoons it feels close to the outdoor temperature, out of the sun, wind and rain.
- **A 4-season glass room is a different project.** If you want a conditioned room you use in August, you are shopping for an addition, and the price reflects that.

## Fixed or folding?

- **Clearfixed** ($31 per sq ft): fixed 4 mm panels, rated to 100 mph, and can be cut into arches and angles.
- **Clearfold** ($40 per sq ft): panels fold to the side so the patio can open back up.
- **Hybrid** ($56 per sq ft): polycarbonate and honeycomb aluminum mesh in one folding system.

Weight and impact resistance are general properties of polycarbonate (Palram Industries), not test results for a complete enclosure.
