# Buyer09: comparison shopper

Houston homeowner with a covered patio, choosing glass, fabric roll-down shade or AE enclosure.

Inspected in both `/workspace/scratch/74495339e6b5/ae-live-baseline` (A) and `/workspace/scratch/74495339e6b5/ae-new-baseline` (B): `src/pages/compare.astro`, `src/pages/index.astro`, `src/data/site.ts`, `src/components/CompareBars.astro`.

A strengths: homepage estimator explicitly prices openings rather than floor area, exposes installation inclusions and extras, then shows comparison totals. Comparison page admits soft mesh costs less and recommends motorized products for convenience/hard-to-reach openings, conditioned glass for August air conditioning, and glass for scratch resistance. Friction: “What the same patio costs” conflates an existing-roof enclosure with a sunroom addition; “Glass can crack or shatter” omits glass-system distinctions.

B improves scope honesty: its glass section says “prices are not like for like,” adds “What gets built,” and acknowledges tempered/laminated glass. Its new retrofit table distinguishes glass enclosure, fabric screens, motorized shades and metal-reinforced AE frames. Homepage tax/unit disclosures are clearer; Clearfixed leads the estimator. However, the comparison hero still promises the “same” patio, and its chart retains the addition-versus-retrofit totals. Neither provides an installed glass-retrofit price or a clearly matched fabric-shade quote. The unsupported “Houston market pricing” $40–$45 rate is not a supplier quotation; named cost guides lack direct evidence links here. B removes homepage comparison bars, reducing immediate scope confusion but placing useful tradeoffs behind a link.

Preferred flow: A's immersive presentation, B's covered-patio comparison before prices, then estimator and measured quote. Keep manual-versus-powered convenience tradeoffs; import B's scope, frame and tax language. Drop universal animal exclusion (“Stops snakes and mice: Yes”) and unqualified payback claims. Preserve B's newer “5 years on parts” as a claim requiring owner confirmation against A's three years.

Highest-impact issue: apparent savings imply equivalent construction. Acceptance: label additions separately at every price comparison; show comparable opening area, scope, tax, exclusions and dated linked evidence, or explicitly mark missing comparator estimates. Fabric shade and insect screen must remain distinct.

Observed: source text/data. Inferred: improved buying confidence. Confidence moderate; no rendered usability or independent pricing verification.
