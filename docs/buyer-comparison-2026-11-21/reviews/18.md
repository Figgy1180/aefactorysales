# Buyer 18 — uncertain fit, no dimensions

Persona/task: Houston homeowner with odd-shaped openings under an existing roof; wants someone to assess feasibility without doing measurements first.

Inspected paths (relative to each snapshot root): A/B `src/pages/index.astro`, `src/pages/patio-enclosures/three-season-rooms.astro`, `src/data/site.ts`; A `src/components/QuoteForm.astro`; B `src/components/BookForm.astro`, `src/pages/book-visit.astro`, `src/pages/free-quote.astro`.

A strengths: Homepage explicitly promises “We measure every opening, check your roof, posts and slab.” Its form accepts “Not sure yet” and “Not sure” size, optional notes, and offers “Book with just your name and phone.” Name/phone/ZIP are required in the main form; email is optional. Existing-roof suitability and shaped fixed panels are present, including a disclosed 1.5× rate for arched/angled fixed panels. Friction: help for irregular openings is scattered; optional photo texting receives little guidance.

B strengths: `/book-visit/` says “Sizes and photos are optional; we measure everything at the visit.” It specifically promises checks of “posts, slopes and gables” and layout design opening by opening. Direct booking bypasses the estimator; the estimator also permits “Not sure.” Its existing-patio explanation distinguishes roof/slab/posts reuse from a new sunroom. Friction: booking requires street address beyond A’s required fields. Actual upload is disabled (`photoUploads: false`); users must text or reply by email. The gable-check steps disappear below 900px. “Book” still means a callback to arrange a time.

Preferred flow: A’s immersive hero with B’s direct, explicitly measurement-free assessment route. Keep A’s roof/posts/slab check and minimal callback; import B’s optional-preparation wording and shape-specific assessment; drop premature suitability certainty (“so it fits the first time”).

Highest-impact issue: distinguish likely suitability from confirmed structural fit. B’s newer limit is “11.5 ft tall (fixed panels and the Hybrid; 10 ft for Hexfold and Clearfold),” versus A’s blanket 10 ft; owner must resolve authorized specifications before merging.

Acceptance check: Mobile visitor can request assessment with dimensions/photos blank, find shape/roof assessment reassurance, understand callback timing and additional shaped-panel cost, and encounter no universal-fit claim.

Observed: source copy, requirements, conditionals. Inferred: B better addresses uncertainty; A reduces initial disclosure friction. Confidence moderate; rendering and submission untested.
