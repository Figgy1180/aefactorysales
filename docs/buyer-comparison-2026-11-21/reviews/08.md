**Persona/task:** Older Houston homeowner with an existing covered patio, comparing a three-season room with metal screens and requesting a visit without pointer precision.

**Inspected paths:** In both `ae-live-baseline/src/` (A) and `ae-new-baseline/src/` (B): `pages/index.astro`, `layouts/Base.astro`, `styles/global.css`, `components/QuoteForm.astro`, `scripts/forms.ts`; additionally A `components/CallMeBack.astro` and B `components/BookForm.astro`.

**A strengths/friction:** A's photo hero establishes the setting, and “Opens by hand” immediately explains operation. Both versions include a skip link, named navigation, native disclosure menus, visible-focus CSS, associated form labels, and invalid-field focus. A's two-action mobile contact bar is simpler. However, the hero's “Book my free site visit” anchor also opens a dialog, making its behavior less predictable from the markup. Full-photo text depends on the gradient and selected image; source cannot establish readability. Snow animates indefinitely unless reduced motion is enabled. Condensed uppercase buttons/headlines add reading effort for this simulated buyer.

**B strengths/friction:** Sentence-case typography, solid-background hero copy, clearer “Book free design visit” versus “Get my estimate now,” grouped booking fields, explicit optional labels and telephone fallback favor comprehension. Dark text on yellow CTAs and cream text on charcoal are defined in CSS; actual contrast and focus visibility require checks. Mobile body text drops to 1rem; three fixed contact actions increase crowding risk. Required street address increases typing compared with A's name/phone shortcut. The visualizer's “drag four corners” instruction suggests a precision-dependent path; alternatives need testing.

**Preferred flow / keep-import-drop:** Keep A's immersive hero with the requested interior photo; import B's sentence case, booking page and clearly distinct estimate path. Keep phone access and a direct systems route. Drop snow and excessive competing hero actions.

**Highest-impact issue:** Preserve a fully keyboard-operable route to understand products and book without requiring the visualizer.

**Acceptance:** At 320px and 200% zoom, keyboard through menu, product choices and booking; verify visible, unobscured focus, readable CTA wrapping, error identification and confirmation. Check reduced motion, including scripted smooth scrolling.

**Evidence limits:** Source observations only; buyer preference and crowding concerns are inferred. No rendered assessment or WCAG compliance claim.
